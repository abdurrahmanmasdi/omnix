# D1 — Market map: Istanbul dental tourism (who, how much, which tools)

_2026-10-06 · Claude (planner). Desk research only. Numbers marked **[official]** come from TÜİK/USHAŞ/Ministry/TDB, **[vendor]** from companies selling to clinics (biased), **[estimate]** is our own calculation. Builds on `OMNA-9_Competitor_Landscape_Brief.md` (competitor list) — not repeated here._

## 1. Bottom line

1. **The market is real but not huge per clinic.** About **300k dental tourists a year** come to Turkey, through about **800 authorized facilities**. An average facility sees **~30–40 international dental patients a month** (~$0.7–2M a year) [estimate]. The top brands see far more.
2. **Clinics pay for leads and people, and both are expensive.** Meta leads cost **$80–180 each** [vendor], only **3–6% of paid-social leads become patients** [vendor], and a coordinator costs **~₺45–70k a month** [job ad]. Every lead we save is worth real money.
3. **Speed is the lever.** Patients message 3–4 clinics. Fast first replies win, and many clinics are slow at night, at weekends and in the patient's language.
4. **The space is crowded with CRMs and inboxes** (Kommo, MetoCRM, respond.io, Invekto…) and a few AI receptionists (STOAIX, AgentFix, Senkron). Their prices: **$79–1,850 a month**. Our edge has to be **the best AI conversation + keeping their own WhatsApp number (coexistence) + TR/EN/AR + safety**, sold as a layer next to what they already use, not as yet another CRM.
5. **Our price hypothesis (7,900–9,900 TL/month) is at the low end** of competitors. We can test higher, value-based pricing once the AI is good.

Patient volume fell from 2023 to 2025 while revenue held, so **spend per patient is rising** (more premium treatments, or fewer budget patients). Competition for each patient is getting tighter.

## 3. Clinic-level economics (what one clinic looks like)

There is **no public revenue data per clinic**: they are private companies (Ltd./A.Ş.) and don't publish financials. This is our best model; D2/D3 will replace it with real answers.

| Item | Typical value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International dental patients per authorized facility | **~350–470 a year (~30–40 a month)** on average; strongly skewed, with the top brands in the thousands | **[estimate]** 280–375k ÷ 800. Example: Maltepe Dental Clinic, "14,000+ patients from 85 countries" over 24 years [vendor PR] |
| Revenue per patient | $2,161 average across all health tourism; dental cosmetic trips $1,500–10,000+ | **[official]** TÜİK; **[vendor]** clinic blogs |
| → International dental revenue per average facility | **~$0.75M–2M a year** | **[estimate]** |
| Cost per lead (Meta/Instagram) | $80–180 | **[vendor]** medicaltourismcrm.com benchmarks (mostly US/EU data) |
| Lead → patient, paid social | 3–6% (referrals 25%+) | **[vendor]** same |
| Patient acquisition cost, dental tourism | $150–500 | **[vendor]** same |
| Coordinator salary | ₺43k–70k a month + bonuses; languages EN/DE/IT | **[job ad]** JFS Health Travel (İzmir), kariyer.net |
| Hiring demand | 44 open "sağlık turizmi satış temsilcisi" jobs in one Istanbul district (Esenyurt) | **[job board]** Glassdoor, Oct 2026 |
| Response-time effect | Contacting within 5 min vs 30 min: ~21× more likely to qualify | **[study]** HBR/InsideSales lead-response research, cited by vendors |

**ROI story for a clinic** [estimate]: 300 leads a month at 5% = 15 patients. Answering every lead in seconds, in their language, day and night, that turns **just 2 more leads a month** into patients is **~$4–8k more revenue a month**, against a tool costing a few hundred dollars. That is the pitch to test in D3.

## 4. What clinics already use, and what it costs

| Product | Type | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MetoCRM** | Turkish health-tourism CRM | **$395–1,850 a month** (3 plans) | Unified inbox (WhatsApp API, IG, FB, Telegram, email, calls), quotes with a visual tooth chart, hotel/transfer operations, commissions; "30,000+ patients yearly"; AI only for analytics |
| **Kommo** | Sales CRM + messaging | per-user plans | Case: Selda Center (Istanbul) — AI chatbot first contact, WhatsApp templates per specialty, AI summaries, "+40% sales" [vendor] |
| **respond.io** | Omnichannel inbox + AI agent | $79–349 a month (+$12–24 per extra user, contact-based overage) | AI agents included in Growth/Advanced |
| **STOAIX** | AI receptionist (calls + WhatsApp) for TR/UK clinics | up to $499 a month (Agency plan), 3-day trial | TR/EN/AR built in; claims 40+ clinics, "8h → 4 min" response, books into calendars |
| **AgentFix** | Turkish AI phone + WhatsApp/IG assistant | on quote | 50+ languages, health tourism, calendar + CRM sync |
| **Senkron** | AI front desk for dental | not public | EN/TR; human handoff; "clinic memory"; no clinical decisions |
| Invekto, MedikalCRM, Chatinbox, ClinicRM, eKlinisyen, ClinicOS, DentClosers, Zellbox, Wati, Callbell | CRM / inbox | see OMNA-9 | Local, crowded |

**What this means:** clinics already buy inboxes and CRMs, so an inbox is not our product. Few products combine **a sales-grade AI conversation** with **the clinic's own WhatsApp number via coexistence** (most need the API number, so the phone app is lost) and **clinic-approved facts with human handoff**. STOAIX is the closest direct competitor to study.

## 5. Implications for OmniX

1. **Positioning:** "Your best coordinator, on your own WhatsApp number, answering every patient in seconds, in their language — and handing the ready ones to your team." Not "a CRM".
2. **Proof we need:** response time (mystery shopping, D2) and the AI conversation quality (AI-1 evals, demo).
3. **Integration over replacement:** many targets run Kommo, MetoCRM or respond.io. Later: export/sync with them (park until a pilot asks).
4. **Pricing test:** 7,900–9,900 TL/month is cheap next to $395+ CRMs and $499 AI receptionists. Test a higher anchor (e.g. a founding price plus a performance story) in D3.
5. **Target first:** mid-size dental-tourism clinics (≈ 20–100 international patients a month) with 1–4 coordinators. Big enough to feel the lead pain, small enough to say yes fast.

## 6. Unknowns → ask in D2/D3
