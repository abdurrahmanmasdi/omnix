# OMNA-10: Istanbul-Only Qualified Lead Pipeline — 25 Clinics

**Prepared for:** Omnix  
**Corrected:** 20 September 2026  
**Geographic scope:** Istanbul, Türkiye only  
**Segments:** premium dentistry, aesthetic/plastic surgery, hair restoration, dermatology and medical tourism

## Executive Summary

This revision replaces the previous UK/UAE/US pipeline. All 25 prospects operate in Istanbul. The list prioritizes clinics where inbound leads arrive through WhatsApp, Instagram, multilingual web forms or medical-tourism coordinators—the workflows OMNIX is designed to qualify, nurture and route.

The first outreach cohort should be **DentSpa, Lema Dental Clinic, Clinic Prime Istanbul, Esnan Dental Clinics, and Vanity Cosmetic Surgery**. They show the clearest combination of premium positioning, international-patient operations, high social/message volume and multi-stage consultation workflows.

## Method and Guardrails

- Public business information only; no patient or private personal data.
- Istanbul presence is mandatory. National brands are included only where an Istanbul operation is visible.
- Scores estimate product fit, not clinical quality.
- Public contact channels and decision-maker titles must be rechecked immediately before outreach.
- Do not claim integration, KVKK compliance or clinical outcomes until verified directly.

## OMNIX-Relevant Buying Signals

1. **WhatsApp is a sales desk, not merely support.** Clinics commonly use photo-based preliminary assessments, quote follow-up, travel coordination and post-treatment check-ins in one thread.
2. **Multilingual coordination is central.** International-patient clinics need language-aware qualification, ownership, follow-up SLAs and safe escalation.
3. **Instagram-to-WhatsApp continuity is a gap.** OMNIX can preserve campaign/source context when a prospect moves from a social ad or DM into a consultation.
4. **High-ticket treatments have long consideration cycles.** Automated, consent-aware follow-up and objection handling can recover leads without replacing clinical judgment.
5. **KVKK and health-data handling are table stakes.** A Turkish launch must use explicit consent, data minimization, access controls, retention rules and human escalation for clinical questions.

## Pilot Recommendation

Offer a narrowly scoped 30-day pilot to 3–5 clinics: one WhatsApp number, one Instagram account, two treatment funnels, Turkish plus English, and CRM export. Measure first-response time, qualified-lead rate, consultation-booking rate, follow-up completion, human handoff rate and opt-out rate. Do not ingest diagnostic images or automate medical advice in the initial pilot.
