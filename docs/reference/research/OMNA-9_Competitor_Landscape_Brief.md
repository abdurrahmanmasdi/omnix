# OMNA-9: Istanbul/Türkiye Competitor Landscape — Clinic Messaging, CRM and AI

**Prepared for:** Omnix  
**Corrected:** 20 September 2026  
**Scope:** Products that an Istanbul dental, medical-aesthetics or medical-tourism clinic could realistically consider

## Executive Summary

The previous brief over-weighted US patient-engagement suites. In Istanbul, OMNIX competes primarily with three groups: **local clinic/health-tourism CRMs** (Invekto, MedikalCRM, ClinicRM/eKlinisyen), **WhatsApp-first inboxes** (Chatinbox, Wati, respond.io, Callbell), and **general sales CRMs** (Kommo). Newer clinic-specific entrants such as ClinicOS, Zellbox and DentClosers make the category more crowded.

The near-term competitive threat is not one dominant platform. It is the clinic's existing bundle of WhatsApp, Instagram, spreadsheets, a local CRM and human coordinators. OMNIX should therefore enter as an integration and conversion layer, with Turkish/English workflows, fast deployment, clear CRM export, KVKK controls and human escalation.

## What Istanbul Buyers Will Compare

1. Turkish-language interface and local onboarding.
2. Official WhatsApp Business Platform support and Instagram DM capture.
3. Multilingual patient conversations, especially Turkish, English, Arabic, German and French.
4. Coordinator ownership, reminders, quote/treatment-plan stages and lost-lead recovery.
5. CRM/PMS interoperability and reliable export—no lock-in.
6. KVKK consent, data location/transfer posture, role-based access, audit logs and retention.
7. Human approval for clinical claims, price commitments and medical advice.
8. Transparent TL pricing and measurable conversion uplift.

## Positioning Recommendation

**OMNIX is the AI conversion and coordination layer for premium Istanbul clinics—not another clinic-management replacement.** It brings WhatsApp and Instagram leads into one patient-aware timeline, qualifies them in their language, answers approved non-clinical objections from clinic-controlled knowledge, and hands sensitive questions to the correct human with full context.

Avoid broad claims such as “autonomous medical receptionist.” Use a safer promise: **faster response, consistent qualification, no missed follow-up, and accountable human handoff.**

## Product Requirements for the Istanbul Pilot

- Turkish and English first; Arabic next.
- One WhatsApp Business number plus Instagram DMs.
- Patient identity merge with explicit human review.
- Clinic-approved RAG sources with versioning and citations in the agent console.
- Hard exclusions for diagnosis, prognosis, contraindications and guaranteed outcomes.
- Consent capture before collecting photos or health information.
- Role-based access, audit trail, retention controls and export/delete workflow.
- CRM webhook/API plus CSV fallback.
- Metrics: response time, qualification rate, booked consultation rate, follow-up completion, handoff rate, opt-out rate.

## 30-Day Competitive Validation

1. Demo Invekto, MedikalCRM, Chatinbox and one of ClinicOS/DentClosers using the same Istanbul dental-tourism scenario.
2. Record exact pricing, onboarding time, WhatsApp/Instagram limitations, data hosting and export options.
3. Run five clinic interviews with treatment coordinators, not only owners.
4. Test OMNIX against a 100-conversation synthetic benchmark in Turkish and English.
5. Publish a battlecard only after vendor claims are directly validated.
