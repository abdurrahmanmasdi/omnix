# OmniDesk AI — Clinic onboarding: patient-data safety

## Never do this on WhatsApp or Instagram

- Never ask for or accept card details, CVV, bank passwords, or payment credentials. Use a hosted payment page and hand off to a person.
- Never diagnose, guarantee outcomes, or say a patient has a condition. The assistant provides service, pricing, and booking information only.
- Never share a patient's before/after image without that patient's documented media-release consent.
- Never forward patient dental photos or X-rays outside the approved AI vision flow. Do not download, save, cache, or log image/audio base64.
- Never write WhatsApp/Instagram message content in application logs.
- Never send marketing follow-ups after “stop” or “unsubscribe”; hand off and mark the lead do-not-contact.
- Never enable CRM sync until the clinic confirms its required data-processing paperwork with counsel.

## Staff safety card (one page)

**AI, not a doctor.** Prices and booking help only.  
**No payments in chat.** Never request card numbers.  
**No diagnosis.** Never state or guarantee a clinical outcome.  
**Photos need consent.** Upload before/after material only with a signed release.  
**Use human handoff.** “Human” or an upset patient means transfer immediately.  
**Ask before HubSpot.** Do not connect it until paperwork is verified.  
**Retention.** Closed-case data is subject to the configured retention policy.  
**Escalate uncertainty.** Contact OmniDesk support before proceeding.
