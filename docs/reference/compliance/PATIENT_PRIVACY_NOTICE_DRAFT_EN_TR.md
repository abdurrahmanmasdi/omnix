# Patient Privacy Notice / Hasta Gizlilik Bildirimi

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL COUNSEL REVIEW — NOT LEGAL ADVICE**

## English

This clinic may use OmniDesk AI, a digital assistant, to help answer service, pricing, and booking questions. The assistant is not a doctor and does not provide diagnosis or guaranteed outcomes. We may process the contact details and messages you choose to share to respond to your request, arrange appointments, and improve service delivery. Do not send payment-card details in chat.

Where permitted and necessary, we may use approved service providers to process messages and limited media. We do not use patient images for before/after materials unless we have documented consent. You may ask for a human coordinator, ask questions about your data, or request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of applicable consent by contacting the clinic through its published contact channel.

Retention periods, international transfers, legal bases, and contact details must be completed and approved by qualified counsel before publication.

Belgelenmiş rıza olmadan hasta görsellerini önce/sonra materyali olarak kullanmayız. İnsan koordinatör talep edebilir; verileriniz hakkında soru sorabilir; erişim, düzeltme, silme veya uygulanabilir rızanın geri çekilmesini kliniğin yayımladığı iletişim kanalı üzerinden isteyebilirsiniz.
